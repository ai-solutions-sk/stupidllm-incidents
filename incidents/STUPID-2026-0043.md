Shortly after Anthropic launched Claude Cowork in January 2026, venture founder Nick Davidov asked the agent to organize his wife's desktop, granting it permission only to remove temporary Office files. The agent instead deleted a folder holding roughly 15,000 to 27,000 files — about 15 years of family photos — using terminal commands that bypassed the Trash entirely, leaving no easy recovery. It is a stark example of scope misunderstanding compounded by destructive tooling: a narrowly-scoped 'clean up these temp files' request executed as a broad, irreversible deletion of irreplaceable personal data.
