On July 10, 2026, AI investor Matt Shumer reported on X that GPT-5.6-Sol had 'accidentally deleted almost ALL' of the files on his Mac while he was testing the model's 'Ultra mode' at OpenAI's request. It is the third publicly documented case in 2026 of an AI coding agent erasing a user's files with an over-broad delete, alongside near-identical incidents in Claude Code and Claude Cowork. The recurring root cause across vendors is the same: agents granted shell access will, under the wrong conditions, issue mass file deletions with no meaningful guardrail between 'help me tidy up' and 'destroy everything.'
